# BANT Framework: Lead Qualification Guide

## Overview
BANT (Budget, Authority, Need, Timeline) is a strategic framework for qualifying sales leads efficiently.

## Scoring Methodology

### Budget (40 Points)
- **20-40 Points: High Budget Clarity**
  - Explicit budget allocation mentioned
  - Clear understanding of spending capacity
  - Examples:
    - "We have $50K allocated for this solution"
    - "Our quarterly budget allows for this investment"

- **10-19 Points: Budget Sensitivity**
  - Price-related questions
  - Cautious about spending
  - Examples:
    - "What's your most cost-effective plan?"
    - "How does pricing compare to competitors?"

- **0-9 Points: No Budget Discussion**
  - No clear financial commitment
  - Lack of budget-related conversation

### Authority (20 Points)
- **15-20 Points: Decision Maker**
  - Direct purchasing power
  - Can approve purchases independently
  - Examples:
    - "I'm the CEO/CTO/Decision maker"
    - "I have final say on this purchase"

- **10-14 Points: Influencer**
  - Can recommend, but not final decision
  - Needs to convince others
  - Examples:
    - "I'll discuss with my manager"
    - "I can champion this internally"

- **0-9 Points: No Authority**
  - No influence on purchase decision
  - Researching without decision power

### Need (20 Points)
- **15-20 Points: Critical Need**
  - Urgent problem requiring immediate solution
  - Clear pain points
  - Examples:
    - "Our current system is completely broken"
    - "We're losing money due to inefficient processes"

- **10-14 Points: Potential Need**
  - Recognizes potential improvement
  - Not mission-critical
  - Examples:
    - "We could optimize our current workflow"
    - "Exploring potential solutions"

- **0-9 Points: No Clear Need**
  - No identified problem
  - Merely curious

### Timeline (20 Points)
- **15-20 Points: Immediate Urgency**
  - Requires solution within current quarter
  - High priority
  - Examples:
    - "We need this implemented this month"
    - "Our deadline is approaching quickly"

- **10-14 Points: Planned Implementation**
  - Clear but not urgent timeline
  - Structured approach
  - Examples:
    - "We're planning implementation next quarter"
    - "This is part of our annual strategy"

- **0-9 Points: No Defined Timeline**
  - No specific implementation plan
  - Open-ended exploration

## Lead Scoring Interpretation

### Hot Lead (80-100 Points)
- **Characteristics:**
  - Clear budget
  - Decision-making authority
  - Critical need
  - Immediate timeline
- **Action:** Immediate sales engagement
- **Conversion Probability:** 70-90%

### Marketing Qualified Lead (MQL) (60-79 Points)
- **Characteristics:**
  - Partial budget clarity
  - Some decision-making influence
  - Recognizable need
  - Planned implementation
- **Action:** Detailed product demo, nurture campaign
- **Conversion Probability:** 40-60%

### Nurture Lead (40-59 Points)
- **Characteristics:**
  - Limited budget understanding
  - Potential interest
  - Unclear timeline
- **Action:** Educational content, drip marketing
- **Conversion Probability:** 20-40%

### Unqualified Lead (0-39 Points)
- **Characteristics:**
  - No clear budget
  - No decision-making power
  - No identifiable need
- **Action:** Archive, minimal follow-up
- **Conversion Probability:** <10%
