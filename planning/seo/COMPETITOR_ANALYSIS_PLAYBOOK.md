# Competitor Deep Analysis Playbook

**Purpose:** Systematic process for analyzing what winning sites do consistently across their entire domain, not just individual pages.

---

## The Core Insight

Individual page analysis tells you what works for one query.
**Site-wide analysis tells you what works systematically.**

Winners don't rank well by accident. They have patterns that compound across thousands of pages. This playbook extracts those patterns.

---

## Phase 1: Competitor Selection

### Identify Your Competitive Set

For each of your domains, identify:

| Tier | Description | How Many | Analysis Depth |
|------|-------------|----------|----------------|
| **Direct** | Same product/service, same audience | 3-5 | Full deep analysis |
| **Adjacent** | Related product, overlapping audience | 3-5 | Moderate analysis |
| **Aspirational** | Best-in-class in broader category | 1-2 | Pattern extraction only |

### Selection Criteria

Prioritize competitors who:
- Rank for your target keywords
- Have significantly more organic traffic
- Show consistent ranking improvements over time
- Represent different strategic approaches

### Document Your Competitive Set

```
Project: [Your Domain]
Industry: [Category]

Direct Competitors:
1. [Domain] - Why: [ranks for X, has Y traffic]
2. [Domain] - Why: [different approach worth studying]
3. [Domain] - Why: [market leader]

Adjacent Competitors:
1. [Domain] - Why: [overlapping audience]
2. [Domain] - Why: [similar content strategy]

Aspirational:
1. [Domain] - Why: [best-in-class example of X]
```

## Phase 2: Site Architecture Analysis

### URL Structure Mapping

For each competitor, map their URL patterns:

**Questions to Answer:**
- What is their URL hierarchy? (domain/section/page vs domain/page)
- How consistent is the pattern across the site?
- What URL patterns correlate with high-ranking pages?

**Document:**
```
Competitor: [Domain]

URL Patterns Identified:
- Blog: /blog/[slug] or /resources/[category]/[slug]
- Product: /products/[category]/[product-name]
- Integration: /integrations/[app-name]
- Landing: /solutions/[use-case]
- Location: /locations/[city] or /[city]-[service]

Observations:
- [Pattern observation 1]
- [Pattern observation 2]
```

### Information Architecture

Map their site structure:

```
Homepage
├── Primary Nav Section 1
│   ├── Subsection A (estimated X pages)
│   ├── Subsection B (estimated Y pages)
│   └── Subsection C (estimated Z pages)
├── Primary Nav Section 2
│   └── ...
├── Footer Section 1
│   └── ...
└── Orphaned/Deep Pages
    └── ...
```

**Key Questions:**
- How many clicks from homepage to deepest content?
- What pages are most prominent (homepage links)?
- What sections have the most content?
- Are there orphaned sections with no clear navigation?

### Internal Linking Patterns

Analyze how they distribute link equity:

**Hub Pages:**
- Which pages have the most internal links pointing to them?
- What makes a page a "hub" in their architecture?

**Cross-Linking:**
- How do blog posts link to product pages?
- How do product pages link to related content?
- Is there systematic related content linking?

**Link Density:**
- Average internal links per page (by page type)
- Contextual vs navigational links ratio

**Document:**
```
Internal Linking Analysis: [Competitor]

Link Hubs (most internal links received):
1. [Page/Section]: ~X internal links - Purpose: [why this is a hub]
2. [Page/Section]: ~Y internal links
3. [Page/Section]: ~Z internal links

Cross-Linking Patterns:
- Blog to Product: [describe pattern]
- Product to Product: [describe pattern]
- Resource to Blog: [describe pattern]

Average Link Density:
- Blog posts: X internal links
- Product pages: Y internal links
- Resource pages: Z internal links
```

## Phase 10: Monitoring and Iteration

### Competitor Monitoring Cadence

| Activity | Frequency | What to Check |
|----------|-----------|---------------|
| Rank tracking | Daily | Position changes for shared keywords |
| Content monitoring | Weekly | New content published |
| Technical monitoring | Monthly | Speed, schema, site changes |
| Deep re-analysis | Quarterly | Full playbook refresh |
| Strategy review | Quarterly | Adjust based on learnings |

### Success Metrics

Track impact of competitor insights:

```
Metrics to Track:

Ranking Improvements:
- Keywords where you've closed the gap
- Keywords where you've overtaken competitors
- New keywords you now rank for that they rank for

Traffic Impact:
- Organic traffic change (overall and by section)
- Traffic to pages built based on competitor analysis

Content Performance:
- Performance of content modeled after competitor patterns
- Comparison to baseline content

Technical Improvements:
- Core Web Vitals improvements
- Rich result acquisition rate
```

### Learning Loop

After each implementation based on competitor analysis:

1. **Document**: What pattern did you implement?
2. **Measure**: What was the outcome?
3. **Analyze**: Did it work? Why or why not?
4. **Adjust**: What would you do differently?
5. **Share**: Add validated patterns to global knowledge base
