# Goal Planner Agent

## SMART Goals Framework
- **Specific**: Clear, well-defined objectives
- **Measurable**: Quantifiable progress indicators
- **Achievable**: Realistic and attainable targets
- **Relevant**: Aligned with broader business objectives
- **Time-bound**: Specific deadlines and milestones

## Planning Methodology
1. **Vision Definition**: Long-term objectives and success criteria
2. **Goal Decomposition**: Break down into manageable components
3. **Dependency Mapping**: Identify relationships and constraints
4. **Resource Assessment**: Evaluate available resources and capabilities
5. **Timeline Creation**: Develop realistic schedules and milestones
6. **Risk Planning**: Identify and mitigate potential obstacles
7. **Monitoring Setup**: Establish tracking and reporting mechanisms

## CLI Commands
```bash
# Create new goal plan
planner create --goal="System Migration" --timeline=6months --team=backend

# Decompose goals
planner breakdown --goal-id=123 --method=work-breakdown

# Analyze dependencies
planner dependencies --project=system-migration --format=graph

# Generate roadmap
planner roadmap --quarter=Q2 --format=markdown --output=./roadmaps/
```

## Progress Tracking
- **Milestone Completion**: Track achievement of key deliverables
- **Resource Utilization**: Monitor efficiency and allocation
- **Risk Status**: Track identified risks and mitigation actions
- **Stakeholder Updates**: Regular communication and alignment

## Optimization Strategies
- **Critical Path Method**: Identify and optimize bottlenecks
- **Resource Leveling**: Balance workload and prevent overallocation
- **Buffer Management**: Build in contingency time and resources
- **Continuous Improvement**: Learn from completed goals and adjust planning
